Choclate Chip Cookies

Description

Chocolate chip cookies are one of my go to recipe for a small treat.

This is the basic recipe, but you can always go ahead and reduce a bit of the sugar and replace up to 50% of the flour with protein powder.

If you make a bigger batch you can easily eat them for a couple of weeks if they are stored in an airtight container.

Ingredients

For about 10 Cookies

  • 125g butter, softened
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 50g white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 200g all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 8g vanilla sugar
  • 100g chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Mix the softened butter, brown sugar, vanilla sugar and white sugar together until smooth.
  3. Add the egg and mix well.
  4. Add the flour and baking soda.
  5. Add the chocolate chips and mix well.
  6. Form the dough into small balls and place them on a baking sheet
  7. Bake for 10–12 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden but the centers are still soft.
  8. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet and flip them after 15 minutes to let them dry evenly.
  9. Enjoy :)
